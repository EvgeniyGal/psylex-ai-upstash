# PsyLex user instructions

PsyLex is a structured mediation platform. AI helps both parties to a dispute find what they truly need and record a voluntary agreement.

> **Legal information, not legal advice.** PsyLex does not replace a lawyer or a psychologist and is not therapy. Participation is voluntary: you may pause or end the process at any time.

---

## Two operating modes

| Mode | Who leads the session | For whom |
|------|----------------------|----------|
| **Mode A: Self-guided** | Mediation AI agent | Party A and Party B without a live mediator |
| **Mode B: For mediators** | A human mediator with AI support | Party A and Party B + mediator |

The mode is set by whoever created the room:

- an **administrator** created the room → Mode A;
- a **mediator** created the room → Mode B.

> **Parties do not choose the mode themselves.** The organizer of the room sets it.

In the app, click **Help** to read these instructions, download PDF / DOCX, or ask the chat assistant.

Administrators and mediators manage participants under **Sides** (mediators: own rooms only; admins: all parties, including roomless). Parties see **Party A** or **Party B** in the header, often with a case title (for example **Party A · Claimant**), and can open **My rooms**.

---

## How to sign in

1. Open the PsyLex home page and click **Login**, **Start on my own**, or **For Mediators →**.
2. Enter the **login** (format `psylex_…`) and **password** sent by the administrator or mediator.
3. Or open a **Magic Link** — in a normal browser, sign-in **completes automatically** (if JavaScript is off — **Continue**). The link is one-time and valid for **72 hours**.

> **Self-registration is not available.** An administrator (Mode A) or a mediator (Mode B) creates the account. A Magic Link can be generated by an admin or by a mediator (for parties in their rooms).

---

## Interface language

The header or sidebar has an **EN | UA** switcher. The selected language is stored. Help, downloads, and chat-assistant replies use the same language.

Ask the Help assistant about waits (toasters), **Sides**, schedule, or Mode A/B.

---

## Short process path (Party A and Party B)

```
Sign in → Welcome → Consent → Tests → Dispute description
       → Lobby (readiness / schedule)
       → Mediation session
       → Voting → (compromise if needed) → Agreement
```

Details of buttons, timers (Mode A: **120 min**, reply time **15 min**), and toasters are in [parties.md](./parties.md). Mediators and administrators have separate consoles — see the matching instructions.
